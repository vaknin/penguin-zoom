Penguin for Zoom

A light Linux desktop app for joining and starting Zoom meetings. It opens Zoom meeting links in a small native window that fits Wayland tiling desktops such as Hyprland, with keyboard controls and global mute and leave keys.

Penguin for Zoom is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed, sponsored or supported by Zoom Communications, Inc. "Zoom" is used only to say which service the app works with. Zoom is a trademark of Zoom Communications, Inc.

What it does

Opens meeting links

Click a Zoom meeting link anywhere, or paste one with Ctrl V, and it opens in Penguin.

Starts meetings

Signed in, press New meeting to start an instant meeting as host, then copy the invite link from the people list.

Join screen

Camera preview, microphone and camera toggles, device choice and your display name before you join.

In the meeting

Mute, camera, screen share, chat, people list, gallery or speaker view, breakout rooms as a participant.

Keyboard first

Single-key controls in the window, and desktop-wide mute and leave keys you can set in any desktop.

Speaker choice

Send meeting audio to the speakers or headphones you pick, without changing the system default.

Little data

No account with us, no analytics, no ads. Settings stay on your computer. See the privacy policy.

How it works

Penguin is a Linux desktop app that shows Zoom's own Meeting SDK for Web inside a WebKitGTK window. Your audio, video, screen share and chat go directly between your computer and Zoom. Zoom's Terms of Service and Privacy Statement apply to the meeting itself.

You sign in with your own Zoom account, and the app joins and starts meetings as you. The only server we run is a small signing service that creates the short-lived join signature Zoom requires. Details are in the privacy policy.

Requirements

  • Arch Linux (or an Arch-based distribution), Ubuntu 24.04 LTS or Fedora 45, with WebKitGTK 4.1, GStreamer and PipeWire or PulseAudio.
  • A Zoom account to sign in with.
  • Works on any Linux desktop; tested on Wayland with Hyprland.

Install

Build it from source for your user (no prebuilt package yet), then start penguin-zoom from your app launcher or a terminal. On Arch:

sudo pacman -S --needed base-devel git rust npm webkit2gtk-4.1 gtk3 gst-plugins-good gst-plugins-bad gst-libav gst-plugin-pipewire libpulse libsecret xdg-utils
git clone https://github.com/vaknin/penguin-zoom ~/.local/src/penguin-zoom
~/.local/src/penguin-zoom/scripts/install-user.sh
penguin-zoom

The package lines for Ubuntu 24.04 and Fedora 45 are in the documentation. Meetings have been tested on Arch; on Ubuntu 24.04 and Fedora 45 the app has been tested to build and start. Full steps, including sign-in, keyboard keys and how to remove the app, are in the documentation.
