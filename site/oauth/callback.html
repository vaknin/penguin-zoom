Penguin for Zoom

Sign in from the app

Penguin for Zoom signs you in from the desktop app: open Penguin, open Settings (the gear at the top right) and press Sign in. After you sign in, Zoom sends your browser here and this page hands the result straight to Penguin on your computer.

This page stores nothing and sends nothing to us. If Penguin isn't running, start it and sign in again. Help: Support.
