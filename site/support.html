Support

Penguin for Zoom is supported by its developer, Aviv Vaknin. Zoom does not provide support for this app; please contact us, not Zoom, about it.

Before you ask

Many problems are covered in troubleshooting. Problems with the meeting itself (a host's settings, your Zoom account, Zoom being down) are for the host or Zoom's support; we can't change those.

What to include

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Your app version (the package version shown by pacman -Q penguin-zoom), your distribution, and your desktop (for example Hyprland on Wayland).
  • If you can, the messages the app prints when started from a terminal with penguin-zoom. Sign-in tokens and signatures are masked in these messages, but check them before posting.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post meeting links, passcodes, email addresses or other personal information there. Send anything private by email instead.

Security problems

Please report security problems privately to dibs.apps@gmail.com, not in a public issue. We will acknowledge them within the same first response time and fix serious problems as a priority.

Privacy requests

To ask what data we hold about you, or to have it deleted, see your rights in the privacy policy, or email dibs.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request".
