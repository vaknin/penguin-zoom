1. What the app is

Penguin is a Linux desktop app for joining Zoom meetings. It shows Zoom's Meeting SDK for Web in a WebKitGTK window and adds its own controls, keyboard keys and settings. Meeting audio, video, screen share and chat go directly between your computer and Zoom. To join, the app gets a join signature from a signing service we run, and, when you sign in, a join token for your Zoom account from Zoom. The documentation describes every feature, and the privacy policy describes exactly what data is used and where it goes.
